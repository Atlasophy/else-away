# The Studio — a guide for Yaren

This is how you change what's on your website. You don't need anyone's help to
do any of it, and you can't break anything permanently.

## Getting in

Go to **elseaway.com/admin**

Type your email address. Cloudflare emails you a six-digit code. Type the code
in, and you're in. There's no password to remember or lose.

The code is only good for a few minutes, and you stay signed in for a day
before it asks again.

## The three sections

Across the top you'll see **Postcards**, **Photographs**, and **Details**.
Whatever you change, nothing goes live until you press **Publish** in the top
right.

### Postcards

The row that drifts sideways near the bottom of the site. Each postcard has a
city, country, approximate time, note, and one or more photographs. The first
photograph becomes the cover; opening the postcard shows the full set.

Use **Add photograph** to choose from your uploaded photographs. **Move up** and
**Move down** change the postcard order. **Remove postcard** removes it from the
draft; it does not disappear from the public site until you publish.

### Photographs

Everything you've uploaded. Upload straight from your camera or phone — the
photographs are made smaller before they're sent, so it works on any
connection and you don't need to resize anything first.

Click a photograph to copy its address if you need it.

### Details

This section controls the contact email, enquiry subject line, Instagram link,
and footer credit shown on the public site. The subject line is filled in
automatically when somebody writes to you.

## Publishing

The **Publish** button wakes up as soon as you change something. Press it and
your changes are live within about a minute.

If something's wrong — for example, a postcard has no place or no photograph —
the studio tells you what needs attention and publishes nothing. Your public
site stays as it was until the draft is valid.

## A few things worth knowing

**Mistakes can be undone.** The site keeps a record of how it was at any point
in the recent past, so if something gets deleted or overwritten by accident it
can be put back. That one isn't a button in the studio — ask whoever looks
after the site and they can restore it in a minute.

**Closing the tab with unpublished changes** will warn you first.

## If something looks broken

Try refreshing the page first. If the studio says it isn't connected, your
internet dropped — nothing was lost, and it'll work again when you're back on.
