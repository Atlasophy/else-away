# The Studio — a guide for Yaren

This is how you change what's on your website. You don't need anyone's help to
do any of it, and you can't break anything permanently.

## Getting in

Go to **elseaway.com/admin**

Type your email address. Cloudflare emails you a six-digit code. Type the code
in, and you're in. There's no password to remember or lose.

The code is only good for a few minutes, and you stay signed in for a day
before it asks again.

## The four sections

Across the top you'll see **Stories**, **Postcards**, **Photographs**, and
**Details**. Whatever you change, nothing goes live until you press
**Publish** in the top right.

### Stories

The seasonal work on your site. Open a season to see its stories.

Each story has:

- **Title** — the name of the piece
- **Place** — where it was, shown under the title on the site
- **Kind** — what sort of place it is. Stay, Café, Maker, Retreat, and so on
- **Location for the map** — this is what the "Explore this place" button
  searches for. Write it the way you'd type it into Google Maps, like
  *Provence France pottery studio*. It doesn't have to be an address
- **Short line** — one sentence. It appears under the title, and on the back
  of any postcard that opens this story
- **The story** — the longer piece of writing

**Add a story** with the button at the bottom of a season. You can have as many
as you like in each season — the site counts them for you.

**Move up / Move down** changes the order they appear in.

**Remove** deletes a story. If a postcard points at it, you'll be warned first.

### Postcards

The row that drifts sideways near the bottom of the site. Each one opens a
story. The written side of the card borrows that story's short line, so you
only ever write it once.

**Opens** is the dropdown that decides which story the card leads to.

### Photographs

Everything you've uploaded. Upload straight from your camera or phone — the
photographs are made smaller before they're sent, so it works on any
connection and you don't need to resize anything first.

Click a photograph to copy its address if you need it.

### Details

Your email address, Instagram link, and the subject line that gets filled in
when somebody writes to you.

## Publishing

The **Publish** button wakes up as soon as you change something. Press it and
your changes are live within about a minute.

If something's wrong — a story with no photograph, two stories with the same
name, a postcard pointing at a story you deleted — it tells you what's wrong
and publishes nothing. Your site stays as it was until it's right.

## A few things worth knowing

**Renaming a story is safe.** Its web address doesn't change when you rename
it, so any link you've already shared keeps working.

**Mistakes can be undone.** The site keeps a record of how it was at any point
in the recent past, so if something gets deleted or overwritten by accident it
can be put back. That one isn't a button in the studio — ask whoever looks
after the site and they can restore it in a minute.

**Closing the tab with unpublished changes** will warn you first.

## If something looks broken

Try refreshing the page first. If the studio says it isn't connected, your
internet dropped — nothing was lost, and it'll work again when you're back on.
